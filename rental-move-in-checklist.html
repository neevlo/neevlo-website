Rental move-in checklist to protect your deposit

Before moving into a rented flat, record the condition of every room with dated photos and share them with your landlord on the first day. Check walls, floors, taps, sockets, doors, appliances and meter readings. This record protects your security deposit when you move out. Neevlo's move-in report costs ₹1,499.

Move-in checklist

How to make the record count

  1. Take photos and a short video of every room in daylight, with the date visible.
  2. List all existing damage in one document.
  3. Email or WhatsApp it to the landlord on move-in day and ask them to reply "received".
  4. Keep it until your deposit is returned.

Is a move-in inspection worth it?

A security deposit is usually many times the ₹1,499 cost of the report, so one avoided deduction pays for it.
