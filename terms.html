Terms of service and refund policy

Last updated: October 2026. In plain language, so you know exactly what to expect.

Who we are

Neevlo ("we") is an independent home inspection service operated by Neevlo Jaipur, Jaipur, Rajasthan. Contact: neevloindia@gmail.com, +91 87640 55535.

What an inspection is

  • A visual and non-destructive check of the parts of the home we can see and reach on the day, using the tools described on our website. We don't drill, cut, lift flooring or open walls.
  • It is not a structural engineering assessment, a legal title check, a pest inspection or a guarantee that the home has no other defects. If we see signs that need a specialist, we say so in the report.
  • Findings reflect the condition on the day of the inspection.

Booking and payment

  • ₹500 confirms your booking. The balance is due after the inspection, before we send the report.
  • Prices are as listed on our pricing page on the day you book. We confirm the final price by home size before the visit.
  • You arrange access to the property, with power and water connected. If we can't get in on the day, we'll reschedule once at no cost.

Rescheduling, cancellations and refunds

  • Reschedule: free, if you tell us at least 24 hours before your slot.
  • Cancel 24+ hours before: the booking amount is refunded in full.
  • Cancel less than 24 hours before: the booking amount is kept, or you can move your slot once instead.
  • If we can't visit for any reason on our side: full refund, or a new slot of your choice.
  • Refunds go back to the original payment method within 7 working days.

The report

  • The report is yours. You can share it with your builder, seller, bank, lawyer or anyone else.
  • Premium reports are sent within 24 hours and Basic reports within 48 hours of the inspection.
  • Suggested fixes are general guidance, not a repair quote or a design for repair work.

Our independence

We never carry out repairs and never accept commissions or referral fees from builders, brokers, contractors or banks.

Liability

We take care to be thorough and accurate. If we fail to report a defect that was visible and within the scope of the inspection on the day, our total liability is limited to the fee you paid for that inspection.

Disputes

Please contact us first and we'll do our best to resolve it. These terms are governed by the laws of India, and courts in Jaipur have jurisdiction.
