Pre-handover flat inspection checklist

Inspect a new flat before you sign the possession letter and pay the last installment. Check dampness, hollow tiles, plumbing, electrical earthing, doors and windows, and carpet area. Under RERA, the builder must fix defects you report within 5 years of possession, within 30 days of your complaint. A written snag list is your proof.

When to inspect

After the builder sends the possession offer, and before you sign the possession letter or pay the final installment. That's when the builder is most willing to fix things.

Do-it-yourself checklist

About 2 hours for a 2 BHK. Take a phone charger, a coin, a bucket and a torch.

Write it down

Take a dated photo of every problem and list them in one document. Send it to the builder by email so you have a record, and keep their acknowledgment.

FAQ

Handover questions

What does the builder have to fix under RERA?

Structural defects and defects in workmanship, quality or services such as plumbing and electrical, reported within 5 years of possession. The builder must fix them within 30 days, at its own cost. Normal wear and tear and your own modifications aren't covered.

Should I take possession if I find defects?

You can accept possession and submit a written snag list at the same time. Keep the builder's acknowledgment. Ask a lawyer if the problems are serious.

What are the most common defects in new flats?

Dampness near bathrooms, hollow floor tiles, poor bathroom floor slope, missing earthing in sockets, and doors or windows that don't close properly.

Can I inspect before the flat is complete?

You can, but final finishing hides and adds problems. The best time is after the possession offer, once the flat is fully finished.
