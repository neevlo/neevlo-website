What we check

Fresh paint hides a lot. We find what you can't see.

Seepage and dampness

Moisture meters and thermal imaging catch leaks hidden behind new paint and tiles.

Cracks and structure

Wall, beam and ceiling cracks, with what's cosmetic and what needs attention.

Electrical safety

Earthing, polarity, loose wiring and overloaded points, socket by socket.

Plumbing and drainage

Water pressure, leaks, drain slope and bathroom waterproofing, tested with water running.

Finishing and fittings

Hollow tiles, doors, windows, fixtures and every small thing a snag list should catch.

Questions

Before you book

Can I be there during the inspection?

Yes, and we encourage it. We'll show you issues as we find them and explain what they mean.

How long does an inspection take?

Usually a few hours for a typical flat. Larger homes and villas take longer.

Do I need permission from the seller or builder?

You'll need access to the property. If it helps, we can speak to the seller or builder on your behalf to arrange it.

Do you also repair the problems you find?

No. We only inspect and report, so we have no reason to exaggerate or hide anything.

Which cities do you cover?

Jaipur, Kota and Udaipur. Buying somewhere else in Rajasthan? Message us and we'll let you know if we can help.
