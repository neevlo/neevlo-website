What we check

Fresh paint hides a lot. We find what you can't see.

Seepage and dampness

A moisture meter and thermal camera catch leaks hidden behind new paint and tiles.

Cracks and structure

Electrical safety

Earthing, polarity, MCB and RCCB, loose wiring and overloaded points, socket by socket.

Plumbing and drainage

Water pressure, leaks, drain slope and bathroom waterproofing, tested with water running.

Carpet area check

We measure the carpet area so you know if you're paying for what was built.

Finishing and fittings

Hollow tiles, doors, windows and fixtures: every small thing a snag list should catch.

Questions

Before you book

How much does a home inspection cost in Jaipur?

A 2 BHK costs ₹4,999 for Basic or ₹6,499 for Premium. Prices start at ₹2,499 for a studio and go up to ₹13,999 for a villa. A rental move-in report costs ₹1,499.

Should I choose Basic or Premium?

Basic covers the most common problems: dampness, hollow tiles, plumbing leaks and wiring faults. Choose Premium if you are taking possession from a builder, because it adds the thermal scan, water-ponding test, full carpet-area measurement and a snag list ready to send to the builder.

How long does an inspection take?

About 2–3 hours for a 2 BHK and 4–5 hours for a villa.

When do I get the report?

Premium reports arrive within 24 hours and Basic reports within 48 hours, on WhatsApp and email. We then call you for 15 minutes to explain the findings.

When do I pay?

₹500 when you book to confirm your slot. You pay the rest after the inspection, before you receive the report.

Can I be there during the inspection?

Yes, and we recommend it. We'll show you the main problems as we find them.

Do I need permission from the builder or seller?

You need access to the property. If it helps, we can speak to the builder or seller on your behalf to arrange it.

Do you also repair the problems you find?

No. We only inspect and report. Because we don't sell repairs or take commissions, we have no reason to exaggerate or hide anything.

Which areas do you cover?

All of Jaipur, at the same prices. Buying somewhere else in Rajasthan? Message us and we will tell you if we can help.
