Our 150-point home inspection checklist

A Neevlo inspection checks 150 points across 11 areas of a home. The largest groups are electrical (22 points), plumbing (20), structure and walls (18), and dampness (16). We use a thermal camera, moisture meter, socket tester, laser meter and tile tap test. Nothing is broken or opened.

The 150 points, by area

AreaPointsWhat we check (examples)
Electrical22Every socket for polarity and earthing, MCB and RCCB working, distribution board labelled, AC and geyser points, exposed or loose wiring
Plumbing and drainage20Water pressure at every tap, leaks under sinks, drain speed, WC flush, concealed pipe leaks, tank and overflow
Structure and walls18Hairline vs structural cracks, beam and column cracks, hollow plaster, walls out of plumb, ceiling sag
Dampness and waterproofing16Moisture readings on walls next to bathrooms, kitchen and outer walls; ceiling under the terrace; paint bubbling; window-sill seepage
Flooring and tiles16Hollow tiles (tap test), cracks, uneven edges, grout gaps, slope towards drains, skirting
Doors and windows16Alignment, locks, hinges, gaps, glass, mesh, sliding tracks, sealant around frames
Bathrooms12Floor slope, water ponding, exhaust fan, fixtures, grout and the corners where leaks start
Kitchen10Platform level, sink drain, chimney and gas pipeline points, tile dado, exhaust
Paint and finishes8Patchy paint, cracks under paint, false-ceiling finish, wardrobe fittings
Balcony, terrace and exterior8Railing height and firmness, drain outlets, slope, parapet cracks
Area and layout4Carpet area against your agreement, room sizes, ceiling height, layout against the approved plan
Total150

Basic and Premium both cover all 150 points. Premium adds the thermal scan, water-ponding test, clamp-meter load check, TDS water test and full laser measurement. Compare packages

Tools we use

  • Thermal camera: shows hidden leaks and damp as cold spots behind walls and ceilings (Premium, or +₹1,500 on Basic).
  • Moisture meter: reads dampness behind paint and tiles.
  • Socket tester: checks earthing and polarity at every socket.
  • Laser distance meter: measures rooms and carpet area.
  • Clamp meter: checks the electrical load on circuits.
  • Spirit level and tile tap tool: check floor slope, platforms and hollow tiles.
  • TDS meter: a quick water-quality reading (Premium).

What we don't do

We don't do structural load or concrete core tests (these need a structural engineer) or legal title checks. Termite checks and water lab tests are coming soon. If we see signs that need a specialist, we tell you in the report.

We also never do repairs or recommend contractors for a commission, so we have no reason to invent or exaggerate a defect.

FAQ

Common questions

Do you check if the building is structurally safe?

We check for visible warning signs such as wide or diagonal cracks, exposed steel and sagging. A full structural safety test needs a structural engineer; we'll flag it if you need one.

Will you damage anything?

No. All checks are non-destructive. We don't drill, cut or open walls.

Can I be there during the inspection?

Yes, and we recommend it. We'll show you the main problems as we find them.

Do you check the society or common areas?

Only areas inside your flat, plus its balcony and any private terrace. Common areas can be added on request.
