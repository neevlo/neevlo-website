Sample home inspection report

A Neevlo report is a PDF of about 20–30 pages, sent on WhatsApp and email (Premium within 24 hours, Basic within 48). It opens with a one-page summary that ranks every problem as Urgent, Fix before possession or Monitor. Each problem has a photo, its location, the reading from our tools and a suggested fix.

What's in the report

SectionWhat it shows
1. SummaryTotal problems found, by severity, on one page
2. Snag listEvery problem with photo, room, measurement and suggested fix
3. Room-by-room resultsPass or fail for each of the 150 points
4. MeasurementsRoom sizes and carpet area compared with your agreement (full laser measurement in Premium)
5. Electrical and water readingsSocket earthing results, MCB test, water pressure, thermal images and TDS (Premium)
6. What to do nextWhich problems to raise with the builder now, and which to watch

Severity levels

Urgent

A safety risk, such as a socket with no earthing. Fix before anyone lives there.

Fix before possession

A defect the builder or seller should repair before you move in.

Monitor

Minor for now. Check again after the monsoon.

FAQ

Report questions

How soon do I get the report?

Premium reports arrive within 24 hours of the inspection and Basic reports within 48 hours, on WhatsApp and email.

Can I send the report to my builder?

Yes. The report is yours. The Premium snag list is formatted so you can forward it directly and keep a record that you reported each problem.

What do the severity levels mean?

Urgent: a safety risk, such as missing earthing. Fix before possession: a defect the builder should repair before you move in. Monitor: minor, but worth checking again after the monsoon.

Is the report in Hindi?

The report is in English. We explain it to you in Hindi or English on a 15-minute call.
