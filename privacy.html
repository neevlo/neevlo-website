Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what information Neevlo ("we") collects through this website, why, and how you can ask us to delete it. Neevlo is operated by Neevlo Jaipur, Jaipur, Rajasthan.

What we collect

  • Enquiry details you type into our booking form: your name, mobile number, locality, property type, inspection type, preferred date and any notes. We save these as you type, including if you don't finish submitting the form, so we can follow up on your enquiry.
  • Your answers to the safety check, if you complete it before sending an enquiry.
  • How you reached the site, such as the referring website, ad campaign tags and whether you're on mobile or desktop, stored with your enquiry.
  • Usage analytics, only if you click Accept on the cookie notice. We use Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to understand which pages are visited and how the site is used. Clarity may record anonymised sessions of clicks and scrolling. Form fields are masked in these recordings.

How we use it

Only to respond to your enquiry, schedule and carry out your inspection, and improve this website. We don't sell your information or share it with builders, brokers or other third parties for marketing.

Where it's stored

Enquiry details are stored in a private Google Sheet accessible only to Neevlo. Analytics data is held by Google and Microsoft under their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that don't become bookings are deleted within 12 months. Customer records are kept only as long as needed for accounting and legal requirements.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information at any time by contacting neevloindia@gmail.com or +91 87640 55535. You can decline analytics cookies on the notice shown on your first visit, or by clearing your browser's site data.
