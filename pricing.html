FAQ

Pricing questions

How much does a home inspection cost in Jaipur?

₹4,999 for a typical 2 BHK with Basic, or ₹6,499 with Premium. The price depends on the size of the home and the package you choose.

Is Basic enough for a new flat?

Basic covers the most common problems: dampness, hollow tiles, plumbing leaks and wiring faults. Choose Premium if you're about to take possession from a builder, because it adds the thermal scan, the ponding test, full area measurement and a builder-ready snag list.

How long does an inspection take?

About 2–3 hours for a 2 BHK and 4–5 hours for a villa.

When do I pay?

₹500 when you book, and the rest after the inspection, before you receive the report. We accept UPI, bank transfer and cash.

Do prices include everything?

Yes. The price shown is the price you pay within Jaipur. There are no hidden charges or travel fees inside the city.

Why are these called launch prices?

We're new in Jaipur, so our first customers get lower prices. Prices will go up later; anything you've already booked stays at the price you were quoted.

Do you inspect outside Jaipur?

Right now we work only in Jaipur. If you are buying elsewhere in Rajasthan, message us and we will tell you if we can help.
